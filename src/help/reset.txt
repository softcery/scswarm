Delete branch swarm/<track>/<id>, its land and fail refs, and those of every
row of the track that depends on it. Delete them on the swarm host, then on
the remote. The next cycle runs the rows again from the `to` tip.

Run it once `status` shows no worker for the row: a running worker writes
the refs again. Pushed commits stay on `to`; revert them there with
`git revert -m 1 <merge commit>`.
