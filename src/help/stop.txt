Write ref refs/swarm/stop/<track>/<id> at the track tip, then mirror it to
the remote. The row reads `stopped` and runs no more until its track branch
moves or `scswarm reset` clears it. Rows after it wait.

A running worker sees the ref within 1 s, kills its try, restarts its
container and exits. Its project does not rest. A waiting, queued or failed
row holds in place. A pushed or merged row refuses: its work sits on `to`.

Run it on the swarm host. A push to the track, for any row, ends the stop.
The row then starts over: work it did before the stop never merges.
